2. What the Engine does

The Engine coordinates requests to multiple language models through supported providers and workflows. It may generate prompt drafts, critique or compare candidate prompts, apply transformations, tune request parameters and present an optimised prompt for the user to review.

Model availability, supported features, scores, latency and costs depend on the selected model, provider, account, configuration and network conditions. Internal scores are heuristic signals; they are not a probability of truth, a certification of quality or a guarantee that a prompt will work equally well across models.
