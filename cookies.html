Browser storage and cookies

Cookie Policy

This policy explains how the Luminary AI Engine website and browser application use cookies, local browser storage and offline-cache technology.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Current implementation: The application source does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. It does use browser storage and service-worker caching for core functionality. Those technologies are not the same thing as cookies.

1. Cookies

A cookie is a small value stored by a website and sent back with later requests to that website. The current repository source does not intentionally create first-party analytics or advertising cookies for the standalone Engine.

Hosting, CDN, security or anti-abuse layers may use strictly operational cookies depending on the active deployment configuration. Third-party websites opened from this site may also set their own cookies under their own policies.

2. Local storage

The standalone application uses browser local storage to retain conversations, settings and the user's OpenRouter API key. The key is encrypted before storage when Web Crypto is available. Local storage remains on the user's browser profile; it is not a cookie and it is not automatically synchronised across devices.

Clearing site data or browser storage can remove saved conversations, settings and credentials. Users can manage or clear this data in their browser's site settings.

3. Service worker and Cache Storage

The site includes a service worker and PWA manifest. The service worker may cache application pages, static resources and fonts in the browser's Cache Storage to support faster loading and offline presentation. Cache Storage is a browser storage API, not a cookie.

4. Third-party services

When you use model functionality, requests are sent to OpenRouter and the selected upstream model provider. Their websites, authentication flows and services may use cookies or other technologies under their own policies. Visiting an external link is subject to that provider's terms and privacy notices.

5. Your choices

  • Use your browser's site settings to view, block or remove cookies and site data.
  • Clear local storage and cached resources to remove locally saved application state; doing so may permanently delete conversation history.
  • Use browser privacy controls or extensions to manage third-party cookies and network requests.

Blocking all site storage may prevent conversation history, saved settings or PWA functionality from working correctly.

6. Changes and contact

If analytics, advertising or other optional cookie-based technologies are introduced in a future release, this policy will be updated to describe their purpose and the available choices before those technologies are used where consent is required.
