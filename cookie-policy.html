Cookie Policy | Luminary AI Engine

This policy explains cookies and similar browser technologies used by Luminary AI Engine, distinguishing essential browser storage from optional analytics and third-party services.

Maintained by Luminary Technicals · Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are browser-stored values. Similar technologies include localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB and service-worker caches. The application may use browser storage for settings, local conversations and a user-supplied API key, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

2. Essential browser storage

Local storage may be needed for application preferences, prompt history or settings. It remains in the browser profile and may be removed by clearing site data. Do not store API keys or private conversations on a shared or untrusted device.

3. Service worker and cache

The site may cache application assets through a service worker to support loading and performance. Cached assets are not a cloud backup of conversations and may remain until updated or cleared.

4. Analytics and third-party technologies

Optional performance telemetry, if enabled, is described in the Privacy Policy. No-Log Mode is enabled by default in the standalone app and disables optional browser performance telemetry. Third-party scripts or advertising, if enabled on a deployment, may use their own technologies under their providers' policies.

5. Managing storage

You can manage or delete cookies and site storage in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect application functionality. Clearing storage may permanently delete locally saved data.

6. Changes

This policy may be revised when browser functionality or integrations change. The update date appears above.
