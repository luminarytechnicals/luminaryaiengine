Domain Policy and Provider Disclosure | Luminary AI Engine

This disclosure explains the relationship between Luminary Technicals, Luminary AI Engine, the dpdns.org namespace and the separate providers that may support the website.

Maintained by Luminary Technicals · Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Publisher and project identity

Luminary AI Engine is a project published and maintained by Luminary Technicals. Its canonical website is luminaryaiengine.dpdns.org. The publisher identity is Luminary Technicals; the domain suffix is not the publisher's name.

2. Domain namespace and provisioning

The website uses a hostname under the dpdns.org namespace. The namespace and underlying domain infrastructure are provider-managed. Use of a subdomain does not mean Luminary Technicals owns or operates the parent namespace, registry, registrar or provider infrastructure. This is a publisher disclosure, not a provider-issued licence, registration certificate or ownership certificate.

3. No affiliation or endorsement

Use of a dpdns.org hostname does not make Luminary Technicals or Luminary AI Engine part of DigitalPlat Domains, DPDNS or any related provider entity. It does not imply affiliation, partnership, sponsorship, approval or endorsement by DigitalPlat, DPDNS or the namespace operator.

4. Separate operational roles

Luminary Technicals is the publisher and project maintainer. DNS and subdomain provisioning are functions of the namespace provider. Website hosting, deployment and edge delivery may be supplied by separate third parties. Source code and project maintenance are associated with the official repository. These roles are distinct.

5. Legal availability

Technical availability may depend on DNS, hosting and network providers. Access may also be limited to comply with legal requests or applicable requirements. At the time of this disclosure, access from India is intentionally restricted in response to legal requests. This does not change the publisher identity.

6. Changes and authoritative sources

Infrastructure arrangements may change. For provider-level domain status, consult the relevant provider. For project identity and source code, consult Luminary Technicals and the official repository.
